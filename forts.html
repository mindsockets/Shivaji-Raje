The Forts

Hundreds of hill-forts held this kingdom together. We can't learn them all — so we learn a core set deeply, meet a few more in passing, and hold the rest as "the idea of hundreds."

The honest count

300+

~240
a careful early count (Sabhasad Bakhar)
~111
built by Shivaji himself
12
now UNESCO World Heritage (July 2025)

Teach the numbers, not every name — that's the honest way to hold "hundreds."

Tier 3 — the idea of hundreds

Beyond the named forts stretch hundreds more, strung along the Sahyadri like beads. A child doesn't memorise them — they feel the number, and the way the whole mountain range became one great fortress. That feeling is the lesson.
