The Sayings Sheet

Every night the child collects one line. Here they all are, in three layers — the famous episode lines, the words of his own identity, and the great war-cries — each with its meaning, its home day, and an honest fact-or-legend tag.

History documented Legend ballad / poetic / popular Context narrative, not a fixed quote Debated genuine era, disputed detail

Layer 1 — the episode lines

The lines that ride on a single scene — the ones a child will shout back at you.

The lineWhat it meansHome
हे राज्य व्हावे ही श्रींची इच्छाHe rājya vhāve hī shrīnchī icchhā "May this kingdom come to be — it is God's own wish." The boyhood oath. Day 5Legend
आधी लगीन कोंढाण्याचं, मग रायबाचंĀdhī lagīn Kondhāṇyāchã, mag Rāybāchã "First the wedding of Kondhana, then my son Rayba's." Tanaji puts the fort before his own son's wedding. Day 17Legend
गड आला, पण सिंह गेलाGaḍ ālā, paṇ sinh gelā "The fort is won, but the lion is gone." Shivaji's lament for the fallen Tanaji. Day 17Legend
होता जिवा म्हणून वाचला शिवाHotā Jivā mhaṇūn vāchlā Shivā "Because there was Jiva, Shiva was saved." Jiva Mahala's blade saves the king at Pratapgad. Day 10Legend
The cannon-signal at VishalgadBaji Prabhu holds Ghod Khind until the guns boom "Hold the pass until you hear the cannon." A remembered scene, not a fixed quotation. Day 12Context
वेडात मराठे वीर दौडले सातVeḍāt Marāṭhe vīr dauḍle sāt "In a fury the seven brave Marathas rode out." Prataprao's charge — a 20th-c. poem by Kusumagraj, not a period quote. Day 19Legend
रयतेच्या भाजीच्या देठालाही हात लावू नकाRayatechyā bhājīchyā deṭhālāhī hāt lāvū nakā "Do not touch even the stalk of a farmer's vegetable." A popular summary of his 1673 Chiplun letter, not an exact quotation. Day 26Context
भूतां परस्परे जडो मैत्र जीवांचेBhūtā paraspare jaḍo maitra jīvānche "May all living beings be joined in friendship." From Dnyaneshwar's Pasaydan (13th c.). Day 27History
वृक्षवल्ली आम्हां सोयरी वनचरेVṛkshavallī āmhā soyarī vanachare "Trees, creepers and forest creatures are our kin." Sant Tukaram. Day 27History
मना सज्जना भक्तिपंथेचि जावेManā sajjanā bhaktipanthechi jāve "O good mind, walk the path of devotion." A famous line from Ramdas's Manache Shlok (the second verse). Day 27History
पाण्यात संताजी-धनाजी दिसतात का?Pāṇyāt Santājī-Dhanājī distāt kā? "Can you see Santaji and Dhanaji in the water?" — said of Mughal horses too frightened to drink. Popular saying. Day 29Tradition

Layer 2 — the lines of who he was

Not battle-cries but identity — the seal on his letters and the names people gave him.

The lineWhat it meansHome
प्रतिपच्चंद्रलेखेव वर्धिष्णुर्विश्ववंदिता, शाहसूनोः शिवस्यैषा मुद्रा भद्राय राजतेThe Rajmudra — his royal seal, in Sanskrit, genuinely of his time "Like the waxing first-day moon, the seal of Shahaji's son Shivaji grows, honoured by the world, shining for the people's good." Kid-version: swarajya starts thin as a crescent and grows. Day 3 · 6History
मर्यादेयं विराजतेMaryādeyaṃ virājate The closing seal on his letters: "here the writing's limit rests." Day 26History
श्रीमंत योगी · जाणता राजा · शिवकल्याण राजाShrīmant Yogī · Jāṇtā Rājā · Shivkalyāṇ Rājā "The wealthy hermit-king," "the king who understands," "the king of the people's welfare" — epithets from a praise-poem traditionally attributed to Samarth Ramdas. Day 26 · 27Debated
…क्षत्रियकुलावतंस, सिंहासनाधीश्वर, महाराजाधिराज छत्रपती शिवाजी महाराज की जय!…Kshatriya-kulāvataṃs, Sinhāsanādhīshwar, Mahārājādhirāj Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj kī jai! The coronation jaykara (proclamation of titles): "…jewel of the warrior line, lord of the throne, great king of kings — victory to Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj!" Largely ceremonial / later-elaborated. Day 22Debated

On the epithets: whether Ramdas was formally Shivaji's guru is debated by historians — and the praise-poem is traditionally accepted as his, though its authenticity is also discussed. See the Ramdas story.

Layer 3 — the war-cries & big words

The words the whole army — and the whole idea — rode on.

The wordWhat it meansHome
हर हर महादेवHar Har Mahādev The traditional battle cry, invoking Shiva. manyHistory
जय भवानी, जय शिवाजीJai Bhavānī, Jai Shivājī "Victory to Bhavani, victory to Shivaji." Popular / later, but beloved. manyLegend
गनिमी कावाGanimī kāvā Guerrilla warfare — hit fast, melt into the hills. His signature tactic. Day 20History
हिंदवी स्वराज्यHindavī Swarājya "Self-rule of this land" — the fuller name of the dream from Day 1. Day 20History
रयतेचं राज्यRayatechã rājya "A kingdom of the common people (the raiyat)" — what his rule was for. Day 26History

⚠ Do NOT teach these as his words

“स्वराज्य हा माझा जन्मसिद्ध हक्क आहे” — "Swarajya is my birthright." This is Lokmanya Tilak, around the 1900s — not Shivaji. Keep it where it belongs.

“शिवाजी न होते तो…” ("Had there been no Shivaji…"). A later Hindi couplet of tribute, not a documented quotation of or by him.
