# Day 4 — The Mavla Brotherhood

*Act 1 — The Dream is Born · `[H]` documented history · the friends who became an army*

**Tonight:** the wild, brave hill-friends who would turn one boy's dream into a band of
brothers — the **Mavlas**.

---

## The telling (English)

You've met the dream, the dreamer, and the mother who taught him. Tonight, meet the friends who
would turn one boy's dream into an army.

As Shivaji grew, he loved to roam the **Maval** — the deep green valleys folded into the
Sahyadri mountains, west of Pune. And there lived the **Mavlas**: hardy hill-people, farmers
and shepherds, who could run up a cliff like mountain goats and knew every rock, stream, and
secret path.

Now, most highborn boys would never mix with such folk. But Shivaji was different. He climbed
with them. He ate with them. He laughed with them and slept under the stars beside them. He
learned their hidden trails and their clever tricks. And they saw something rare in him — that
he treated them as **equals**, as brothers, never as servants.

One by one, the bravest hearts came to him. There was **Tanaji Malusare**, fearless as a lion.
There was **Kanhoji Jedhe**, who brought whole clans of warriors along with him. There was
**Baji Pasalkar**, strong and wise. They weren't a hired army marching for coins — they were
*friends* who would follow him anywhere: up any wall, through any dark, into any danger.

Without quite saying the words, they made a promise to one another: *We are one. Your fight is
our fight. Whatever comes, we climb it together.*

This band of mountain brothers would one day storm forts in the black of night, hold narrow
passes to their last breath, and win a whole kingdom from the greatest empires in the land. But
it began so simply — with a boy who chose his friends by their courage and their heart, and
never by how they were born.

And knowing every hidden fold of these hills? That, too, would one day become a secret weapon
with a name — **ganimi kava** — but that is a story for a later night.

So tonight there is no battle. Just boys on a green hillside, becoming brothers.

*Goodnight — tonight's badge: **Mavle, "the brotherhood."***

## गोष्ट (मराठी)

तू स्वप्न भेटलास, स्वप्न पाहणारा भेटलास, आणि त्याला घडवणारी आईही भेटली. आज भेटूया त्या मित्रांना,
जे एका मुलाच्या स्वप्नाचं सैन्यात रूपांतर करणार होते.

शिवाजी मोठा होत गेला, तसा त्याला **मावळ** भटकायला फार आवडायचं — पुण्याच्या पश्चिमेला,
सह्याद्रीच्या घडीत लपलेल्या त्या हिरव्यागार दऱ्या. आणि तिथे राहायचे **मावळे**: काटक डोंगरी माणसं,
शेतकरी आणि धनगर, जे डोंगरी बकऱ्यांसारखे कडा चढून जायचे आणि प्रत्येक दगड, ओढा नि गुप्त वाट
ज्यांना ठाऊक होती.

आता, उच्च घराण्यातली बहुतेक मुलं अशा माणसांत कधी मिसळली नसती. पण शिवाजी वेगळा होता. तो
त्यांच्यासोबत कडे चढायचा. त्यांच्यासोबत जेवायचा. त्यांच्यासोबत हसायचा आणि ताऱ्यांखाली झोपायचा.
त्यांच्या लपलेल्या वाटा आणि हुशार युक्त्या तो शिकला. आणि त्यांनाही त्याच्यात काहीतरी दुर्मिळ दिसलं —
की तो त्यांना **बरोबरीने** वागवतो, भाऊ म्हणून, नोकर म्हणून कधीच नाही.

एकेक करत शूर मनं त्याच्याकडे आली. **तानाजी मालुसरे** — सिंहासारखा निर्भय. **कान्होजी जेधे** —
ज्याने अख्खी योद्ध्यांची घराणी सोबत आणली. **बाजी पासलकर** — बलवान आणि शहाणा. ते पैशासाठी
कूच करणारं भाडोत्री सैन्य नव्हतं — ते *मित्र* होते, जे त्याच्यामागे कुठेही जातील: कोणत्याही तटावर,
कोणत्याही अंधारातून, कोणत्याही संकटात.

शब्दांत न सांगताच त्यांनी एकमेकांना वचन दिलं: *आपण एक आहोत. तुझी लढाई ती आमची लढाई. काहीही
येवो, आपण मिळून त्यावर चढून जाऊ.*

हे डोंगरी भावांचं टोळकं एक दिवस काळोख्या रात्री किल्ले सर करणार होतं, अरुंद खिंडी शेवटच्या
श्वासापर्यंत लढवणार होतं, आणि साऱ्या बलाढ्य साम्राज्यांकडून अख्खं राज्य जिंकून घेणार होतं. पण
सुरुवात इतकी साधी होती — एक मुलगा, जो मित्र निवडायचा त्यांच्या धाडसाने आणि मनाने, कधीच त्यांच्या
जन्माने नाही.

आणि या डोंगरांची प्रत्येक लपलेली घडी ठाऊक असणं? तेही एक दिवस एका गुप्त शस्त्रात बदलणार होतं —
**गनिमी कावा** — पण ती गोष्ट पुढच्या एका रात्रीची.

तर आज रात्री युद्ध नाही. फक्त हिरव्या डोंगरउतारावरची मुलं, भाऊ होत चाललेली.

*शुभ रात्री — आजचं जपायचं नाणं: **मावळे, "भावांचं टोळकं."***

*उद्या रात्री: एका लपलेल्या डोंगरी देवळातली गुप्त शपथ.*

## Fact vs legend

- `[H]` **History:** The **Mavlas** — the people of the **Maval** valleys west of Pune — really
  were the backbone of Shivaji's early forces: light, fast infantry with unmatched knowledge of the
  terrain. Early companions such as **Tanaji Malusare**, **Kanhoji Jedhe**, and **Baji Pasalkar**
  are historical, and their loyalty was central to his rise.
- `[C]` **Framing:** the specific boyhood scenes (climbing, sharing meals, the unspoken vow) are a
  warm reconstruction — but Shivaji's genuinely unusual bond with common hill-folk, and leadership
  by loyalty rather than birth alone, are well remembered.
- **Bridge:** their terrain mastery is the seed of **ganimi kava** (guerrilla warfare), told in
  full on **Day 20**.
