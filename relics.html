The Relics

The weapons of the great deeds, the regalia of the crown, and the places you can still visit today. For almost every "original" there's an authenticity debate — we tell it warmly but honestly: arguing which is the real one is part of the fun.

Weapons of the great deeds

Wagh Nakh वाघनखंDay 10
The "tiger claws" of the Afzal Khan encounter. A set is at the Victoria & Albert Museum, London; it came to India on a 3-year loan for the 350th anniversary, shown from 19 Jul 2024 at the Satara museum, then rotating. Debated — some say the V&A set isn't the authentic one (the "real" one said to be in Satara with the royal descendants); the V&A itself notes no firm proof it was Shivaji's.
Bichwa बिचवाDay 10
The second, hidden dagger — the "scorpion" blade — kept ready at Pratapgad.
The sword तलवारDay 22
Three are popularly named — Bhavani, Jagdamba, Tulja — and which is "the" sword is debated. The Jagdamba Talwar is at the V&A, and Maharashtra has sought its return. Debated

⚠ The honest caveat
